Hi!
I'm Amelia.

I am a sophomore Diplomacy and International Relations major with a Minor in Spanish. I grew up in the suburbs of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. History has always been fascinating to me, especially American History. This project became a passion for me as I dug through Seton Hall's Archives. I was able to dive into the history of one of my communities and explore a topic that had never been deeply explored.

Thank you to the Monsignor Field Archives for establishing the Times Machines programs and thank you to Professor Fieldston for advising my research. Also, a special thank you to Trenton Stevens for helping to develop this website and to Elijah Novak for helping with the research process.
